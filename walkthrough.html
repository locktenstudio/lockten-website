How it works

Three steps. About ten minutes.

01

Walk

Open the app at a job site. Tap a room. Take photos of what needs to be done.

02

Talk

Voice memo each item as you walk. "Caulk this seam." "Replace this pull." The app handles the formatting.

03

Preview the PDF. Edit if you want. Text or email it to your sub from your own phone, in their normal thread.

The difference

What makes Walkthrough different.

Your subs don't need accounts.

Reports send from your own phone as a text or email, in the thread your sub already uses. They open the PDF. Done. No portal to learn, no password to forget, no "click here to view."

Photos go to your Drive or Dropbox.

Pictures save straight to your Google Drive or Dropbox, organized by report. Your camera roll stays clean. That bathroom photo from March is in the Cherry Lane Punch Walk folder.

Voice-first, not type-first.

Talk while you walk. The app handles transcription and formatting. Builders are not going to type into a phone on a job site.

You edit before you send.

The AI gives you a starting point. You make it yours. The PDF that goes out is the version you approved, not the version a black box generated.

Questions

Things people ask.

Do my subs need to download anything?

No. They get a text or email from you with a PDF attached. They open the PDF. That's it.

Can I edit the report before it sends?

Yes. Every walkthrough produces a PDF you preview and edit before sending. The AI gives you a starting point. You decide what goes out.

Where do the photos go?

If you've connected Google Drive or Dropbox, photos save there in a folder for that walkthrough. If you haven't, they save in the app and you can connect Drive or Dropbox later to back-fill.

Does Walkthrough work on Android?

iOS is first, at launch. The Android version follows within a few weeks.

How does sign-in work? I don't want another password.

You enter your email. We email you a six-digit code. You type it in. You're signed in. No password to remember or reset.

What if I want to use this with my whole team?

Each person gets their own seat at $24.99 a month. They sign in with their own email, and reports they send come from their own phone. The subscription handles the seats; there's nothing extra to set up.

Can I try it before I subscribe?

Three free walkthroughs to start, no credit card. After the third, the subscription page comes up. Before then, no card.

What if I cancel?

Cancel anytime from Settings. You keep what you've paid through that month. If you cancel within the first 14 days and it's not working for you, reply to the welcome email and we'll refund.
