How it works

Three steps. About ten minutes.

01

Walk

Open the app at a job site. Tap a room. Take photos of what needs to be done.

02

Talk

Voice memo each item as you walk. "Caulk this seam." "Replace this pull." The app handles the formatting.

03

Send

Preview the PDF. Edit if you want. Text or email it to your sub from your own phone, in their normal thread.

Daily log

The daily log, talked out on the drive home.

Nobody wants to write up the day at six o'clock. Talk it through instead. Walkthrough dates it, pulls the day's weather for that address from the National Weather Service, attaches your photos and builds the PDF. Lose time to weather and you can mark the day a partial or full delay, with a note, the day it happens.

Daily log is on the Standard plan, and it counts as one of your three free reports.

Questions

Things people ask.

Do my subs need to download anything?

No. They get a text or email from you with a PDF attached. They open the PDF. That's it.

Can I edit the report before it sends?

Yes. Every walkthrough produces a PDF you preview and edit before sending. The AI gives you a starting point. You decide what goes out.

Where do the photos go?

If you've connected Google Drive or Dropbox, photos save there in a folder for that walkthrough. If you haven't, they save in the app and you can connect Drive or Dropbox later to back-fill.

Does Walkthrough do daily logs?

Yes. Talk through the day at the end of it and you get a dated, photo-backed daily log with the weather already filled in and a weather-delay flag if you lost time. It is on the Standard plan.

Where does the weather on a daily log come from?

The National Weather Service, for the address on that project and the date on that log. The report prints where the reading came from, and you can edit it before you send.

Does Walkthrough work on Android?

Yes. Walkthrough is on Google Play and on the App Store. Sign in with the same email on either one and your projects and reports are there.

How does sign-in work? I don't want another password.

You enter your email. We email you a six-digit code. You type it in. You're signed in. No password to remember or reset.

What are the plans and what do they cost?

Two plans, month to month. Standard is $9.99 a user each month, or $99.99 a year, and includes every report type except meeting notes and the punch list, with unlimited reports. Pro is $24.99 a user each month, or $239.99 a year, and adds meeting notes and the persistent punch list. You can move from Standard to Pro anytime.

What if I want to use this with my whole team?

Each person gets their own seat, Standard at $9.99 a month or Pro at $24.99. They sign in with their own email, and reports they send come from their own phone. The subscription handles the seats; there's nothing extra to set up.

Can I try it before I subscribe?

Three free reports to start, with every feature unlocked and no credit card. Use them on any report type you like, meeting notes and punch list included. After the third report, you pick a plan. Before then, no card.

What if I cancel?

Cancel anytime from Settings. You keep what you've paid through that month. If you cancel within the first 14 days and it's not working for you, reply to the welcome email and we'll refund.
