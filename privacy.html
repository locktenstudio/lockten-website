Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Lock Ten Studio LLC ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Products this policy covers

This is the single privacy policy for all Lock Ten Studio products:

  • Walkthrough: Site Reports (iOS app for builders)
  • Architect Walkthrough (iOS app for architects)
  • The Install (setup and onboarding service)
  • Lock Ten Field Notes (email newsletter)

Walkthrough: Site Reports and Architect Walkthrough work the same way and handle your data the same way; where this policy says "the apps," it means both.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Email address when you subscribe to our newsletter (Lock Ten Field Notes) or contact us
  • Account information if you sign up for one of the apps (name, email, business name) or The Install (name, email, business details provided in the intake questionnaire)
  • Content you capture in the apps such as photos, voice narration, the transcripts, reports, and punch lists generated from them, and project notes. Most voice capture is transcribed into text on your device; the audio itself never leaves your phone. Recorded meetings are the exception: that audio is uploaded and sent to a third-party transcription service to produce text. Your transcripts are sent to a third-party AI service to format documents
  • Location in two limited ways, both optional: photos you take can include where they were taken, and the apps can use your device's location to notice you've arrived at a project site and offer to open your punch list. That arrival check happens on your device
  • Usage information such as which features you use and when, tied to your account, so we can understand what's working and improve the product
  • Payment information processed through Stripe (web purchases) or through Apple as an in-app purchase; we do not store credit card details on our servers

How we use information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Deliver the products and services you've requested (newsletter, the apps, The Install setup)
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process payments through Stripe
  • Improve our products and communicate about updates or related offerings

Sharing of information

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your content to train AI models. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help operate our business: Beehiiv (newsletter email), Resend (app sign-in emails), Stripe and Apple (payments), RevenueCat (subscription management), Render (hosting), Cloudflare R2 (file storage for photos, audio, and reports), AssemblyAI (meeting audio transcription), Anthropic (AI report formatting), and Sentry (crash diagnostics). Each receives only the data it needs to perform its function
  • As required by law or to protect our rights

Email communications

If you subscribe to Lock Ten Field Notes or other Lock Ten Studio communications, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. We use Beehiiv to deliver newsletter content.

Your choices

You can:

  • Unsubscribe from any email communication using the link in the email
  • Request access to or deletion of your information by emailing info@lockten.ai
  • Decline to provide information, though some features may be unavailable as a result

Data retention

We keep your account information and the content you capture for as long as your account is active, so the app can show you your past work. We do not keep it indefinitely after you leave: when you delete your account, we remove your personal information and your captured content within 30 days. Server logs that may contain IP addresses rotate on a standard 30-day cycle. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics that cannot be traced back to you may be kept longer.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; we do our best.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about how we handle your information: info@lockten.ai
