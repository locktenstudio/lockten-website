What you get

Stop chasing orders. Start the day knowing where everything stands.

Finish materials are the part of the job that lives in email. Confirmations, ship notices, backorder notes, the rep saying "Monday." Material Monitor reads all of it so nobody in your office has to.

Vendor emails become status updates

The ship notice, the tracking number, the backorder note. It reads them and proposes the change, with the vendor's own words quoted. You confirm with one tap instead of retyping anything.

The quiet orders get surfaced

The cabinetry ordered three weeks ago with no timeline. The tile whose firm date came and went. They show up on your list before they show up as a hole in the schedule.

Follow-ups written, ready to send

Pick the items that need a word and it writes one email per rep with every open order on it, models and dates filled in. You read it and send it from your own address, in your own name.

The field checks things in from a phone

A delivery lands, the super taps what arrived, how many, in what condition, with a photo of the packing slip. The office sees it as soon as it is saved, and the morning email carries it the next day.

How it works

Three steps, and none of them belong to your vendors.

Nobody outside your office has to change a thing. Your reps keep sending the same emails they send today.

01

Point your vendor mail at it

Forward or copy the confirmations you already get to a private address that is yours alone, or connect the inbox they land in. Bring your selections list from Buildertrend, JobTread or a spreadsheet, and it knows what to watch for.

02

It reads. A person confirms.

Every email becomes a proposed change waiting for someone in your office: ordered, shipped, backordered, a new delivery date. Confirm it or dismiss it. Only an exact tracking-number match applies by itself.

03

Every morning, one email

Problems first. Then what landed, in the receiver's own words. Then the orders that have gone quiet, with the follow-ups already drafted. Everything else collapses into one grey line at the bottom.

Questions

Things builders ask.

Do my vendors have to do anything?

No. They keep emailing you exactly the way they do now. There is no portal for them, nothing to sign up for and no new address to learn. Material Monitor works from the mail your office already receives.

Do I need Buildertrend?

No. Your selections list from Buildertrend, JobTread or a spreadsheet becomes the list of items it watches, and anything outside that list you add in a line. It does not log in to any other system.

Does it email my vendors?

Never on its own. When an order needs a word, it drafts the follow-up, one email per rep with every open item on it, and you send it from your own address in your own words. Your reps hear from you, not from a system.

What happens when it reads something wrong?

Everything it reads becomes a proposal with the vendor's own words quoted beside it, and a person confirms or dismisses it. Nothing changes on its own, with one exception: an email whose tracking number exactly matches an item already carrying that number updates that item automatically.

Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Both. Forwarding vendor mail to your private Material Monitor address works with any email system. Connecting an inbox directly is being built with the founding builders, Microsoft 365 first.
