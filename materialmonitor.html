Know what to order and when

One email every morning

The crew works the same list

Shipments followed with the carrier

The field app

The crew carries the same list on site.

The field app is the crew's half of Material Monitor, for the superintendent or whoever is on site when the delivery comes. What they record on site is in front of the office right away, and what the office changes shows up on their phones.

Receive a delivery.

Check off what came, mark anything short, damaged or wrong, note where it was put and add a photo.

Look up a material.

Search any job by room, material or model number and see where it stands, when it is due and who the rep is.

Report a problem.

Missing, wrong, damaged or needed for an inspection. A sentence goes to the office by email and stays on their board until it is handled.

There is an iPhone app for the crew. The office invites each person from the console, and they sign in with a six digit code sent to their email.

The morning email

One email each morning with what needs attention.

Everyone on the team who wants it gets one email each morning: what is waiting for an OK, what has to be ordered, what was flagged, what arrived and what vendors and carriers reported. The office's own changes are counted in one gray line at the bottom, and on a day with nothing to report, no email goes out.

Who decides

It proposes. A person decides.

A wrong status turns into a problem on site, so Material Monitor suggests changes and leaves the decision to somebody in your office.

It reads the inboxes you already have.

Vendor mail comes into real people's inboxes, so you can connect or forward several and label each one: the coordinator's, the project manager's, the estimator's, an in-house designer's or a shared one. It only asks about a new sender when a message looks like an order, a shipment, a delivery or a quote.

Every suggestion waits for a yes.

Everything else it reads becomes a suggestion with the vendor's sentence quoted beside it, and a person confirms or dismisses it. No email and no carrier can mark a material received.

Common questions

Things builders ask.

Do my vendors have to do anything?

No. They keep emailing the same people they email now. There is no portal for them, nothing to sign up for and no new address to learn.

Do I need Buildertrend?

No. Each job's list comes in from a spreadsheet, a PDF or a selections export from the software you already use, and anything missing you add in a line. It works beside Buildertrend if you have it, and it never logs in to any other system.

Can my crew use it on site?

Yes. The field app is the crew's half of the same list, on an iPhone: they receive deliveries, look up any material on any job and report problems to the office. The office invites them from the console, and they sign in with a six digit code sent to their email.

Whose inbox does it read?

Only the ones your office connects or forwards from. Vendor mail usually comes into real people's inboxes, like the coordinator's, the project manager's, the estimator's or an in-house designer's. Some offices also have a shared one. You can connect several and give each one a name. It only reads. It never sends, replies, moves or deletes anything, and it only asks about a new sender when a message looks like an order, a shipment, a delivery or a quote.

Microsoft 365 or Google?

Both. Material Monitor works with Microsoft 365 and Google inboxes, by connecting the inbox or by forwarding from it to your own private Material Monitor address.

Does it email my vendors?

Never on its own. When an order needs a word, it drafts the follow-up, one email per rep with every open item on it, and you send it from your own email in your own words. Your reps hear from you.

Does it track shipments?

Yes. Once a material has a tracking number, Material Monitor checks it with the carrier each day and keeps the expected date current. If the carrier reports a problem, the material is flagged as delayed and the office hears about it. When the carrier says it was delivered, somebody still confirms it arrived.

What happens when it reads something wrong?

You dismiss it and nothing changes. Two things update by themselves, and only on a material that already has a tracking number: a shipping notice in a connected inbox, which can set the tracking, the expected date and Shipped, and the carrier's own tracking, which can move the expected date or flag a delay. Everything else it reads becomes a suggestion with the vendor's sentence quoted beside it, and a person confirms or dismisses it. No email and no carrier can mark a material received.

How does an office get set up?

Somebody in the office signs in at app.materialmonitor.app with a work email and a six digit code, names the company, adds the jobs, brings in each job's selections from a spreadsheet or an export and invites the team. Connecting vendor mail is the last step, and the list works without it.
