The studio

Made by a builder, tested on his own jobs.

Lock Ten Studio is run by a custom home builder in Cabin John, Maryland. Each of its AI tools is made for his own company first, and used on real jobs there before it is offered to anyone else.

That is why the tools are small and specific. Each one takes on a piece of the work you already do, fits the way you already run a week and asks you to adopt nothing else. If it does not hold up on a job, it gets fixed before anyone else sees it.
