Walkthrough

Walk the site, talk, and send a finished report.

Built first for builders, and used by designers, stagers, subs and architects too. Anyone who works on or in a house.

  1. 01

    Talk instead of typing.

    Walk the job, take pictures and say what needs to get done. Walkthrough writes it up.

  2. 02

    A clean PDF with your name on it.

    Set your logo, your color and your company name once, and every report carries your brand instead of ours.

  3. 03

    Sent from your own phone.

    Text or email the report to your sub in your normal thread. No portal, and no account for them to make. Photos save to your own Google Drive or Dropbox, not your camera roll.

Pricing

Three free reports to start, no card. After that, Standard is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year, and Pro is $24.99 a month or $239.99 a year, per user. Pro adds meeting notes and a punch list you reopen and reshare as it shrinks.

The studio

Made by a builder, tested on his own jobs.

Lock Ten Studio is run by a custom home builder in Cabin John, Maryland. Each of its AI tools is made for his own company first, and used on real jobs there before it is offered to anyone else.

That is why the tools are small and specific. Each one takes on a piece of the work you already do, fits the way you already run a week and asks you to adopt nothing else. If it does not hold up on a job, it gets fixed before anyone else sees it.
